\begin{abstract}
We report a sequence learner that is not autoregressive. It is
\emph{challenge--response}: one input per tick, one output per tick, and
intervals in which the input is the identity and the model keeps emitting. Its
two operations are inverses selected by the world --- speech \emph{binds} an
observation into a superposed store, silence \emph{unbinds} one link out of it
--- so that depth is neither a layer count nor a hyperparameter but the number
of quiet ticks the world grants. Several partial responses mature in parallel
and the strongest is said; what was said is heard back, and bound into the key
of what the world did next. There is no reward, no label, and no gradient across
a memory write. We verify in isolation that a chain is traversed one link per
quiet tick and halts at its end, derive and confirm the store's operating
region $d>2k\ln V$, show that binding is invertible only for unitary codebooks,
and show that locality-sensitive addressing makes a near miss undetectable by
construction. We measure without a single number: learners are ordered by the
facts they maintain, codelength is read as an instrument against a control
that cannot accumulate, and sources are screened with memoryless judges before
a model sees them. On a synthetic continual stream binding, superposition and
the event itself in the state are load-bearing; on 1.73M intensive-care
measurements the memory accumulates where a bounded context cannot, and its
one weakness against a counter is a rate, not a price. Components built on top
of the mechanism --- a routed operator graph, a read-back check, an exact
per-context trace, a situation-bound episodic store --- are reported with what
they maintained and why they are left out; the last recalls a recurring
situation across long gaps but not from one exposure. Every evaluation here is
open-loop, so none tests the response half of the design. We argue that no
static source can, set out what a world must have to test it --- its own clock,
influence without generation, recurring laws, persistent situations ---
explain why a learner in it still needs no reward, and name where the design might be useful once it has been tested there.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

\subsection{Three commitments of the autoregressive frame}

The dominant frame for sequence learning factorises
$p(x_{1:T})=\prod_t p(x_t\mid x_{<t})$, feeds a prefix, and predicts the next
symbol \citep{elman1990finding,vaswani2017attention}. It has been extraordinarily
productive, and it carries three commitments that are easy to stop seeing
because everything around them assumes the same ones.

\paragraph{The objective is next-symbol prediction.} Every metric derived from
it inherits that shape. A system evaluated by per-symbol codelength is being
asked how well it does the thing autoregression is for, whatever else it may
have been built to do.

\paragraph{Computation per symbol is fixed.} A forward pass costs what it costs
whether the world gives the model a microsecond or an hour. Depth is a property
of the architecture, chosen at design time. Recent stateful models
\citep{gu2023mamba} keep a running state rather than a window, but the
computation per token is still fixed and the objective is still next-symbol.

\paragraph{The context is a prefix.} An exact, recent, ordered window.
Long-range association is bought by widening it \citep{beltagy2020longformer,%
dai2019transformerxl} or by retrieving into it \citep{borgeaud2022improving,%
weston2015memory}. Association at distance is thereby made a question about how
much text you can hold, rather than about what you have stored.

For a system meant to run in a real temporal world --- embodied
\citep{brooks1991intelligence}, or agentic over long horizons --- all three sit
badly. There is no prefix boundary in a room. Computation that cannot use extra
time cannot exploit a lull, and lulls are most of what a real stream contains.
And an exact window is the wrong idealisation for a background that in any
organism is a fuzzy, multi-timescale trace rather than a transcript
\citep{brainerd2002fuzzy}.

\subsection{What we put in their place}

We take challenge--response as the primitive. There is no ``a challenge arrives,
now respond'' --- that phrasing is already the autoregressive shape, one prompt
one completion. There is a stream, and every tick carries one input and produces
one output. Some inputs are the world's tokens; in the intervals between them the
input is the identity and the model still emits, iterating on memory. The
model's own emissions are part of its context, interleaved with the world's:
distinguished from them, and combined with them.

The picture we have kept in view is two people talking. While the other is still
speaking you have already begun to think; when they stop, or just as they stop,
you begin to say something. That interval is the gap. What is forming in it is
\emph{not one trajectory} --- several half-formed replies mature together and one
of them is what comes out. Each clause of that sentence turned out to be
load-bearing, and each had to be recovered separately after a run in which the
mechanism measured inert (\S\ref{sec:lessons}).

The mechanism follows from taking the identity input seriously. If speech means
``there is something to combine'', then silence means ``there is not'', and what
is called for is the inverse operation. Binding writes; unbinding reads; the tick
already says which. Nothing is scheduled, and --- crucially --- nothing needs to
know whether a question is outstanding.

\subsection{What is borrowed}

Storage is content-addressed through a fixed map that no later learning can
move, following the position argued in \citet{yang_2026_22161096}: allocation
buys freedom from interference and pays for it in retrieval difficulty, and the
weakness of the family is that discrimination between addresses decays as
addresses crowd. That trade organises our design, and one of our results is that
in our implementation half of the crowding penalty was self-inflicted
(\S\ref{sec:addressing}).

The representation is holographic \citep{plate1995holographic} in the vector
symbolic tradition \citep{smolensky1990tensor,kanerva1988sparse,%
kanerva2009hyperdimensional,gayler2003vector,eliasmith2013build,%
kleyko2023survey}: binding by circular convolution, reading by circular
correlation, cleanup against a codebook. The readout is a delta rule
\citep{widrow1960adaptive,rosenblatt1958perceptron}. The continual setting ---
regimes arriving, running and departing permanently, with no task identifier
and no boundary signal --- is the one in which catastrophic interference was
characterised \citep{mccloskey1989catastrophic,french1999catastrophic} and to
which complementary-systems and regularisation accounts respond
\citep{mcclelland1995complementary,kirkpatrick2017overcoming,%
parisi2019continual}.

\subsection{What this paper reports, and what it cannot}

The mechanism of \S\ref{sec:design} is the one we stand behind. Several
components were built on top of it, measured, and are kept in the
implementation as switches that are off; \S\ref{sec:explore} reports each with
what it maintained and why it is not part of the mechanism. That is where a
routed operator graph, a read-back check on the emission, an exact per-context
trace and a situation-bound episodic store are discussed.

Every evaluation we have run is \emph{open-loop}: the world's next event is
fixed in advance and does not depend on what the model said. That tests memory
and prediction. It does not test the response half of challenge--response ---
what is said, and what the world does because it was said --- and
\S\ref{sec:closing} argues that no static source can. The evaluation this design
most needs is a world that runs on its own clock and that the learner's
behaviour influences without generating, such as a simulated physical
environment; we set out what such a world must have and where the design might
be useful once it has been tested there.

\subsection{Contributions}

\begin{enumerate}
\item A tick in which binding and unbinding are two halves of one loop selected
by whether the world spoke, so that gap periods, self-output-as-context, and
depth-from-time are consequences of a single rule rather than three mechanisms
(\S\ref{sec:design}, Figure~\ref{fig:tick}).

\item An isolated demonstration that a chain is traversed one link per quiet
tick, halts at its end, and is unharmed by surplus silence
(\S\ref{sec:isolated}, Figure~\ref{fig:walk}).

\item The operating region of a superposed store, $d>2k\ln V$, derived and
confirmed, with the observation that circular correlation inverts convolution
only for unitary codebooks --- a Gaussian codebook loses almost half the signal
with a single item stored (\S\ref{sec:capacity}).

\item A structural result: locality-sensitive addressing makes a near miss
undetectable \emph{by construction}. Addressing needs same-content-same-bank and
gains nothing from similar-content-similar-bank (\S\ref{sec:addressing}).

\item A measurement method without a single scalar: learners ordered by the
facts they maintain, prequential codelength read as an instrument and never as
the objective, proper scores for answers that carry no information, and a screen
that decides whether a source can show anything before a model sees it
(\S\ref{sec:measure}).

\item Results on a synthetic continual stream and on a real clinical stream,
including where the design loses and why (\S\ref{sec:synthetic},
\S\ref{sec:clinical}), and a record of what was tried on top of the mechanism
and not kept (\S\ref{sec:explore}).

\item An account of what static data cannot test, the properties a world must
have to test the rest, and candidate applications (\S\ref{sec:closing}).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Philosophical commitments}
\label{sec:philosophy}

These are not decoration. Each translates into a constraint that the
implementation is checked against, and several of our negative results are
consequences of holding to them.

\subsection{Intelligence as the organisation of memory, not as optimisation
against a scalar}

The founding position of this work is that intelligence is an emergence of
memory rather than a product of reward. The contrast is with the framing in
which behaviour is whatever maximises a scalar
\citep{sutton2018reinforcement,silver2021reward}; here there is no reward, no
value function, and no policy. This is the paper's central claim and it is meant
literally: nothing in the learner is maximised, no scalar reaches a memory
write, and no quantity summarises how well the learner is doing. A companion
mathematical study \citep{yang2026gluing} gives the reason in a form that does
not depend on taste. Ordering learners by the facts they maintain, a faithful
scalar score exists only if that order is total, and asking for ``one number''
is asking for a choice of linear extension that the facts do not determine; a
reward-free learner therefore has no scalar metric, and reintroducing one
reintroduces the objective it gave up. When \S\ref{sec:closing} turns to what
would make a learner move in a world, the answer it gives is not a reward under
another name, and it says why. It is closer to the tradition in which memory is
the computational substrate rather than a subsystem attached to one
\citep{hebb1949organization,tulving1972episodic,gallistel2009memory}.

The design consequence is sharp: the ledger may say what was surprising, and may
therefore decide \emph{what gets written and how deeply}, but it may not be
descended. No gradient crosses a memory write. A node performs a write, not a
fit. The readout's delta rule is the one learner the design sanctions, and it is
error-driven rather than frequency-driven (\S\ref{sec:compression}). Anything
else fitted to the charge is a departure that has to argue for itself, and in
\S\ref{sec:explore} one such departure is reported and left out for that reason.

A test we apply before adopting any change follows from this: \emph{would it
still be right if the charge got worse?} A change justified only by a lower
codelength is a change that has made the ledger the objective.

\subsection{Compression as the currency, without frequency as the organiser}
\label{sec:compression}

We charge prequential codelength \citep{dawid1984present,rissanen1978modeling,%
blier2018description}, in the tradition that identifies compression with
understanding \citep{solomonoff1964formal,hutter2005universal}. But we
deliberately do not organise memory by frequency, and the reason is a claim
about scale rather than about statistics.

\begin{remark}[Why counts degrade with data]
A per-location count distribution $N(o\mid u)/N(u)$ is a frequency estimate.
As a stream accumulates, the count distribution at any location converges toward
the corpus marginal --- it becomes the global unigram. A counted prior therefore
\emph{stops discriminating exactly as the data grows}: it degrades under
scale, which is the same shape of failure as address crowding.
\end{remark}

More data tends toward disorder; what makes it mean something is organisation,
and organisation need not be a Bayesian prior \citep{jaynes2003probability}.
The delta rule already \emph{is} an organiser of the right kind: it is
error-driven, so a row holds what needed correcting here rather than what was
common here, and a token already predicted stops updating. Counts accumulate and
degrade under growth; the delta rule converges and is stable under it. There are
therefore no counts and no escape chain in the mechanism, and the graceful
degradation they used to provide is assigned to the dynamics rather than to a
smoothing rule.

\subsection{An imprecise background is the point, not a compromise}

The background is a fuzzy multi-timescale trace. It is meant to say \emph{which
memories are relevant here}, not to reconstruct what was said. We state as a
non-goal that precise context association is not what this system is for: a
design that makes the background exact has rebuilt the prefix under another
name, and inherits its costs. The nearest cognitive analogue is fuzzy-trace
theory's distinction between verbatim and gist representations
\citep{brainerd2002fuzzy}; the nearest engineering analogue is that a
multi-timescale reservoir is a context, not a transcript
\citep{jaeger2001echo}. This commitment decided one of the explorations of
\S\ref{sec:explore}: an exact per-context store answers a class of questions the
mechanism cannot, and is left out because it is exactly what this commitment
excludes.

\subsection{Time as a resource, and the ledger that must not be gameable}

If depth is bought with the world's silence, then a system's efficiency is part
of its capability, and evaluation must be conducted where silences are real. A
closely related commitment concerns the shape of the world. If the agent's
actions \emph{generated} its observations, surprise could be minimised by
finding a quiet corner --- the dark-room objection to naive free-energy accounts
\citep{friston2010free,friston2012free,clark2013whatever}. The escape is not a
patch but a property of reality: the world proceeds whether or not the agent
attends, so behaviour \emph{influences} what is experienced without
\emph{generating} it, and missing something costs you later. No evaluation in
this paper has that property; \S\ref{sec:closing} returns to it.

\subsection{No single number}

A continual learner can be compared with another only by what it does, and what
it does is many things. We order learners by inclusion of the facts they
maintain --- properties with known answers, each checked against a stated
threshold --- and report the order rather than a score. A faithful scalar exists
only if that order is total, and the least number of scalars that represents it
is its order dimension \citep{dushnik1941partially}; ``give one number'' asks
for a choice the facts do not make. Where we report codelength, accuracy or
AUROC, they are readings of particular instruments, never the verdict on a
learner (\S\ref{sec:measure}).

\subsection{Awe, and the limits of a constructed test}

We take seriously that intelligence is not a quantity our sources can put under
strain. A constructed stream can isolate mechanisms; it cannot decide whether
those mechanisms amount to anything. A replayed real stream is closer to the
world and still cannot answer back. We have tried to write the negative results
as what they are --- observations consistent with a plausible mechanism, on
sources we argue cannot settle them --- rather than as verdicts, and to say for
each what it redirects us toward. Where the design should be judged is set out
in \S\ref{sec:closing}, and we expect that judgement to be considerably harder
than anything reported here.

\section{Design}
\label{sec:design}

\subsection{Notation and the algebra}

Let $d$ be the width, $\CB=\{E_o\}_{o=1}^{V}\subset\mathbb{R}^d$ the fixed
codebook, $\nu(\cdot)$ normalisation to the unit sphere, and $\bind$ circular
convolution,
\begin{equation}
(a\bind b)_k \;=\; \sum_{j=0}^{d-1} a_j\, b_{(k-j)\bmod d}.
\end{equation}
Write $a^{*}$ for the involution $a^{*}_0=a_0$, $a^{*}_j=a_{d-j}$, and
$m \unbind a \;=\; m \bind a^{*}$ for circular correlation.

\begin{definition}[Unitary codebook]
$a\in\mathbb{R}^d$ is \emph{unitary} if $|\hat a_j|=1$ for every Fourier
coefficient $\hat a_j$. We construct $\CB$ by drawing phases uniformly and
taking an inverse transform, so every $E_o$ is unitary.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Exact inversion]
\label{prop:inverse}
For unitary $a$, $\;a\bind a^{*}=\delta$, hence $(a\bind b)\unbind a=b$ exactly.
For general $a$, $(a\bind b)\unbind a = b\bind(a\bind a^{*})$, and $a\bind a^{*}$
is a $\delta$ only in expectation.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Sketch]
Convolution is multiplication in the Fourier domain and the involution conjugates,
so $\widehat{a\bind a^{*}}_j=|\hat a_j|^2$. This is identically one exactly when
$a$ is unitary; for Gaussian $a$ the $|\hat a_j|^2$ are $\chi^2$-distributed with
substantial variance, and the reconstruction is degraded before any interference.
\end{proof}

Proposition~\ref{prop:inverse} is not a technicality. Table~\ref{tab:unitary}
measures the cost: with a \emph{single} triple stored and no interference
whatever, a Gaussian codebook reconstructs at cosine $0.527$.

Write $x_{-1},x_{-2}$ for the two previous observed tokens, $c_i$ for the state
of the $i$-th response, $\tau_i$ for the codebook identity it has resolved to,
$w_i\in[0,1]$ for how live it is, and $h(\cdot,\cdot)$ for a hash of two token
identities into $\{1,\dots,B\}$.

\paragraph{Speech binds.}
\begin{equation}
\Mem_{h(\tau_{x_{-2}},\,\tau_{x_{-1}})}
\;\mathrel{+}=\;
\nrm{\big(E_{x_{-2}}\bind E_{x_{-1}}\big)\bind E_{x_t}} .
\label{eq:write}
\end{equation}
For a presented link $x\;r\;y$ the triple formed at the $y$ tick is exactly
$(E_x\bind E_r)\bind E_y$, and no episode boundary was needed to know that. The
sum is \emph{not} renormalised: $\Mem\leftarrow\nu(\Mem+t)$ shrinks everything
already stored by $1/\sqrt2$ per write, giving the first item weight $2^{-k/2}$
--- a sliding window, which is the one thing a superposition must not be.

\paragraph{Silence unbinds; and so does speech.} On \emph{every} tick each
response advances one link:
\begin{align}
q_i &= \nrm{c_i \bind E_{\text{arr}}}, &
r_i &= \nrm{\Mem_{h(\tau_i,\tau_{\text{arr}})} \unbind q_i}, \label{eq:read}\\
\tau_i' &= \arg\max_{o\in\CB} \langle E_o, r_i\rangle, &
c_i &\leftarrow E_{\tau_i'} \ \text{ iff } \
\langle E_{\tau_i'},r_i\rangle > \theta(d,V). \label{eq:cleanup}
\end{align}
Cleanup ranges over the whole codebook: naming a token cannot require that it
has been learned before. Because $\bind$ commutes, a response that has just
reached $a_1$ after hearing $r$ carries $E_{a_1}\bind E_r$ as its next key with
no bookkeeping. This is where the model's own output does its work: the
response \emph{is} the position reached, and the relation stays on the table as
the operator.

\paragraph{Arrival holds; speech supersedes.}
\begin{equation}
w_i \leftarrow
\begin{cases}
1 & \text{cleanup accepted},\\
\gamma\, w_i & \text{rejected and the response has never advanced},\\
w_i & \text{rejected and it has advanced at least once (it has \emph{arrived})},
\end{cases}
\label{eq:weight}
\end{equation}
and on every event tick $w_i\leftarrow\gamma w_i$ for all $i$ before a new
response is seeded at the observed token. From inside, ``the chain ended'' and
``the chain never started'' are identical; the only thing separating them is
whether anything was ever retrieved. Holding a half-formed reply while the other
person is quiet, and dropping it when they say something new, is the same
picture.

\paragraph{The emission reads the most matured response.} Let
$i^{\star}=\arg\max_i (w_i, \text{recency}_i)$ lexicographically. Then
$p=c_{i^\star}$, and the state is pulled toward the background on every read,
$p\leftarrow\nu\big(p+\alpha\sum_k b_k\big)$ with $b_k$ the background bands of
\S\ref{sec:background}. The features are
$\varphi=[\,p\,;\,E_x\,;\,\text{bound traces}\,;\,\text{event-locked bands}\,]$,
where the bound traces are $E_{x_{-j}}\bind E_x$ for the two previous events and
$b_k\bind E_x$ for each band, and
\begin{equation}
s(o) \;=\; \underbrace{\langle R_o,\varphi\rangle}_{\text{learned}}
\;+\;\lambda\underbrace{\langle E_o,p\rangle}_{\text{cleanup}} .
\label{eq:score}
\end{equation}
The event itself, $E_x$, is the lag-zero member of the family the bound traces
form, since $E_x\bind\delta=E_x$; without it a conjunction is present without
its parts, and a new pair of familiar parts cannot be answered from the parts.
Scores are normalised by subtracting their maximum, never by clamping them:
the rows are unregularised and grow, and a clamp makes every large score equal.

\begin{remark}[Why not the mixture]
Averaging unit responses and renormalising divides each by
$\|\sum_i w_i c_i\|\approx\sqrt{\sum_i w_i^2}$. Measured, the response holding
the answer arrived at the readout at $0.24$--$0.28$ against a codebook floor of
$0.255$: present and indistinguishable from noise. ``Several half-formed answers
mature together and one of them is said'' is not ``say their average''. Reading
the strongest is the natural readout of parallel responses, not a competition
between mechanisms.
\end{remark}

\paragraph{The write.} Rows are updated by a delta rule
\begin{equation}
R_o \mathrel{+}= \eta\,\big(\mathbb{1}[o=x]-q(o)\big)\,\varphi \qquad \text{for every } o,
\label{eq:delta}
\end{equation}
against the emitted distribution $q$ --- the distribution the ledger charged.
The negatives are therefore what the model would actually have said, weighted
by how much it would have said them. Truncating them to the few tokens ranked
highest looks like the same principle and is not: on a stream whose right
charge for a token of probability $p$ is exactly $-\log_2p$, sixteen negatives
leave tokens rarer than $2^{-10}$ with a probability near $2^{-50}$
(\S\ref{sec:measure}). The step $\eta$ is fixed.

\subsection{Three streams}
\label{sec:streams}

The model hears itself. Three self channels are fed back every tick, each
through its own fixed rotation so that they do not superpose into the world's
channel, and each only into the fastest band of the background (A5 below):
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{covert} --- what it currently takes the answer to be, emitted every
tick because the system is always outputting;
\item \emph{overt} --- what it has said out loud, once the evidence stops
moving;
\item \emph{write} --- which memory it is touching: a fixed vector for the bank
the response last unbound.
\end{itemize}
These streams are context, not content: they reach the features and the
fastest band, and nothing they carry is ever stored as having happened.

What was said reaches memory once, in the key and never in the value. When the
world says $x$ after the model said $a$ (and $x\neq a$), a second triple is
written under the pair (the world's token before $a$, $a$ marked as said),
with value $x$:
\begin{equation}
\Mem_{h(\tau_{x_{-1}},\,\hat\tau_a)} \;\mathrel{+}=\;
\nrm{\big(E_{x_{-1}}\bind (S\bind E_a)\big)\bind E_{x}},
\label{eq:said}
\end{equation}
where $S$ is a fixed unitary vector and $\hat\tau_a$ an identity in a space of
its own. The mark matters. The silent read above uses the last thing said as
its relation, so an unmarked $(x_{-1},a)$ is exactly the key a response at
$x_{-1}$ reads: a finished walk then read ``what the world did after I said
$a$ here'' and walked off its answer. An act is heard back on its own channel,
not as the world's word. Nothing in the mechanism reads these keys yet; they
are what a world that reacts would be learned from (\S\ref{sec:closing}).

\subsection{Structural constraints}

Five constraints are asserted by unit tests that run on every build.

\begin{description}
\item[A1 Rate limit.] Constant computation per tick. The only way to think
longer is to occupy more ticks, and ticks are given by the world.
\item[A2 Event-driven accounting.] Charged only when the world speaks, so ``the
identity input injects no new content'' is a consequence of the ledger rather
than an added axiom.
\item[A3 Read/write separation.] The write is content addressed and
deterministic; reads may wander.
\item[A4 No content write at baseline.] Identity ticks write nothing, asserted
bit-for-bit over 500 baseline ticks.
\item[A5 Timescale reachability asymmetry.] The world writes all rungs; the
model's own output reaches only the fastest, so self-generated content
contributes exactly zero to the slow timescales --- a structural ceiling on
confabulation. What was said enters memory only as a marked key, never as a
stored value.
\end{description}

\subsection{The background, and why its key is frozen}
\label{sec:background}

The background is a cascaded band-pass ladder: one use of a ladder, on the time
axis only. Three rungs span one to 256 ticks log-uniformly. The memory itself is
flat, because a ladder inside the store would be an abuse of the device.

The bands are snapshotted \emph{at the last event}, so the retrieval key stops
moving when the world does. The reason is that the bands carry two things at
once: which regime this is, which must hold across a silence, and how long the
world has been quiet, which changes every tick. With both in the key, elapsed
time decides whether a memory fires at all --- measured, a fact learned at six
ticks of silence did not answer at one, scoring $0.000$ where the same pair
presented as an ordinary fact scored $0.74$. A clock in the key is a bug; the
live fast rung remains available to the commit rule, which is where elapsed time
belongs.

\subsection{What is not in the mechanism}

There is no routed operator graph: an earlier design passed the state through a
learned transform chosen by content from a small-world graph. Its memory is not
inert, but on every reading we have, removing it costs nothing and keeping it
costs something (\S\ref{sec:explore}). There is no count, no escape chain, no
reward, no task boundary and no replay buffer. Further components exist in the
implementation as switches that are off, each reported in \S\ref{sec:explore}:
a read-back check on the emission, a gate on silent hops that return to the
token just heard, a stream reporting where the last write went, a per-context
exact trace, a situation-bound episodic store, and step-size rules.

\subsection{Capacity, and the operating region}
\label{sec:capacity}

\begin{proposition}[Operating region]
\label{prop:capacity}
Let $\Mem=\sum_{i\le k}t_i$ with $t_i$ unit and mutually near-orthogonal, and
let cleanup take the best match among $V$ codebook entries in $\mathbb{R}^d$.
Then a stored triple returns $\langle\hat\Mem,t_j\rangle\approx k^{-1/2}$, an
unwritten key returns $\approx d^{-1/2}$, and a random vector's best codebook
match is $\approx\sqrt{2\ln V/d}$. Retrieval is usable when
\begin{equation}
\frac{1}{\sqrt{k}} \;>\; \sqrt{\frac{2\ln V}{d}}
\qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad
d \;>\; 2k\ln V .
\label{eq:capacity}
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}

At $d=256$ we measure cosine $0.043$ with $k=401$ against a predicted $0.050$.
Equation~\eqref{eq:capacity} is what gives content addressing a job that is not
about accuracy: the address chooses \emph{which} superposition to unbind, each
bank stays under capacity, and the read stays one unbinding however much has
been stored. Related capacity analyses for distributed representations appear in
\citet{plate1995holographic} and \citet{frady2018theory}.

It also fixes the cleanup threshold, which must not be a hand-set constant:
$\theta(d,V)=\kappa\sqrt{2\ln V/d}$. A hand-set $0.25$ once sat against a floor
of $0.452$ --- \emph{below} the noise --- so cleanup accepted noise on $97.6\%$
of silent ticks and the response was randomised every tick while the instrument
reported near-perfect success.

\subsection{Addressing must not preserve locality}
\label{sec:addressing}

A key nobody wrote still hashes into an occupied bank, and its occupants answer.
The end of a chain therefore does not retrieve \emph{nothing}; it retrieves a
neighbour. No threshold separates the two, because both are real stored content
of comparable strength. This is address crowding \citep{yang_2026_22161096} in
its sharpest form.

A read-back check was built for it --- bind the candidate back onto the key and
ask whether that triple is in the bank at all --- and it failed instructively.

\begin{lemma}[Locality defeats verification]
\label{lem:lsh}
Under sign-bit locality-sensitive hashing
\citep{indyk1998approximate,charikar2002similarity}, the occupants of a bank
have keys close to the query. If cleanup returns some occupant's target $y_i$
then, for unitary vectors,
\[
\big\langle \nrm{p_i\bind y_i},\ \nrm{q\bind y_i}\big\rangle=\langle p_i,q\rangle,
\]
and $\langle p_i,q\rangle$ being large is \emph{precisely why} $p_i$ and $q$
share a bank. The verification is defeated by the property it was built to
detect.
\end{lemma}

Addressing needs same-content-same-bank and gains nothing from
similar-content-similar-bank; the second property is pure cost and it is what
makes a near miss undetectable. Scrambling the code removes the locality but
makes the address brittle, since write and read reach the same convolution by
different floating-point paths and one flipped sign then means an unrelated
bank. That dilemma is false: cleanup has already resolved the response to an
exact codebook token and the write holds both identities, so the address can be
a hash of the \emph{identities}. Vectors carry the superposition; identities
carry the address. With that in place the read-back check becomes inert on the
walk --- zero rejections. Its later use on the emission is reported in
\S\ref{sec:explore}.

\section{How we measure without a single number}
\label{sec:measure}

A design with no objective still has to be measured, and every instrument we
used can be mistaken for an objective. This section fixes what each instrument
is for before any result is reported.

\subsection{Codelength is an instrument}

We charge prequential codelength on the ticks where the world speaks: predict,
pay $-\log_2 q(x)$, then write \citep{dawid1984present,blier2018description}.
It is the currency of \S\ref{sec:compression}, and it is a reading taken inside
a counter's frame --- the codelength of the next token, which is the
autoregressive target this design refuses. It is useful for one thing: a margin
that moves when a mechanism is repaired says the repair reached something real.
We report PPM \citep{cleary1984data} beside it not as a standard to beat but as
a control that \emph{cannot accumulate}: a bounded context carries nothing
across itself, so a sustained gap is evidence of accumulation. A number
maximised is a reward, so no change is adopted because it lowers codelength
alone.

\subsection{Known answers for the instruments themselves}

Several of our worst errors were in instruments, not mechanisms
(\S\ref{sec:lessons}). An instrument is therefore first run where its answer is
known. Two examples recur below.
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Rarity.} On an i.i.d.\ Zipf stream the right charge for a token of
probability $p$ is exactly $-\log_2 p$, so the excess over it, split by rarity,
must be flat. A readout that writes off what it has seen rarely shows as excess
growing with rarity. Writing against sixteen negatives gave an excess of
$57.0$ bits on tokens rarer than $2^{-10}$; writing against the whole
emitted distribution, $0.47$.
\item \emph{Comparators.} An online counting comparator first scored below
chance on a clinical outcome, because a feature with no variance yet observed
was standardised against a scale floor of $10^{-6}$. A planted linear signal
now has to be found (AUROC above $0.8$) before the comparator is used.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Facts, not a score}

Learners are compared by the facts they maintain (Table~\ref{tab:facts}). Each
fact has a known answer on a stream built for it and a stated threshold; a
learner either maintains it or does not, and the measured value is reported
beside the verdict. Learners are then ordered by inclusion: $a\geq b$ when $a$
maintains every fact $b$ maintains. We report the incomparable pairs, the width
of the order and bounds on its dimension; where the order is not total, no
single number is faithful to it, and the least number of scalars that represents
it is its order dimension \citep{dushnik1941partially,yang2026gluing}.

\subsection{Proper scores for answers with nothing under them}

An answer given with no information behind it is correct about half the time
at a 0.5 threshold whatever it does, so accuracy on it is noise. We score such
answers with the Brier score and expected calibration error
\citep{brier1950verification}. The distinction decided one exploration: read by
accuracy, the silence after a question appeared to make an ungrounded answer
worse, and a gate was built to stop it; read by proper scores, the silence makes
it \emph{better}, and the gate was withdrawn (F9; \S\ref{sec:explore}).

\subsection{Screening a source before a model sees it}

If the last few events, counts, or counts with elapsed time already answer a
question, no state can show anything on that question, however good it is. So
before a source is used we score memoryless judges on it --- last-$k$ events,
bags of counts, counts with elapsed time, and for a retrieval question the rule
that answers it by lookup --- and use the source only if the question needs
what they lack. Three sources failed or passed this screen in instructive ways
(Appendix~\ref{app:screens}).

\subsection{One probe for both arms, and comparators in the same regime}

Two mechanisms compared through a readout must be compared through the same
readout, with the same regularisation sweep, on the same items. And a
comparator must learn in the same regime as the model --- online, one pass,
each item predicted before it is learned from --- or the comparison measures
the regime. Both rules were learned by breaking them (\S\ref{sec:lessons}).

The alternative reading, that the answer is simply too rare to learn, is

The converse error is also available and was once made: a residual on a
deterministic stream read as this misadjustment. On a stream whose map is
replaced every $10^5$ events, the residual in the last tenth before a change
\emph{shrinks} as the step grows ($0.024$ at step $0.5$, $0.037$ at $0.2$, $0.085$
at $0.05$), whereas a misadjustment grows with it, and recovery after the change
is slower at the smaller step ($9768$ against $20486$ events). On a stationary
stream of $6\times10^5$ events the residual is still falling in the last segment
at every step ($0.0080\to0.0065$ at $0.2$). It is a descent in progress, not a
floor.

Two rules that shrink the step as a row settles were measured on the same
changing stream. A step of $\eta/(1+n)$ never learns ($5.17$ bits in the first
segment, $4.81$ in the tenth, against $0.97$ and $0.04$ for the fixed step). An
error-driven step learns ($4.47\to0.58$) but converges an order of magnitude
worse than the fixed step and recovers no better ($6.23$ bits after the change,
against $1.38$). Neither can tell ``the error is small because this row has
learned'' from ``the error is small because it is nearly there''.

\section{A real stream: intensive-care measurements}
\label{sec:clinical}

\emph{This section is not a benchmark claim. It records what one real,
irregularly timed stream says about the mechanism, and where it cannot say
anything. Every figure is from the mechanism as described; the clinical runs
are a single pass over a fixed data set, so seed variation enters only through
the codebook and is small beside the effects reported.}

\subsection{Data, and why this source}

PhysioNet/CinC Challenge 2012 \citep{silva2012predicting}, set-a: 3996
intensive-care stays, 1.73M timestamped measurements. Each measurement is one
token (parameter, octile of its value), $V=296$. Elapsed time between
measurements enters as idle ticks, $\lfloor\log_2\rfloor$ of the gap in minutes,
capped at 11, so the world's silence is the world's own clock. In-hospital
death: 554 stays (13.9\%). The source passed the screen of
\S\ref{sec:measure}: the observation times carry information of their own
(Appendix~\ref{app:screens}). Memory runs continually across all stays and is
never reset; only the volatile state --- responses in flight, the background
cascade --- restarts per stay.

The unit of prediction had to be chosen, and the choice decides what is
measured. At the granularity of a panel (the set of parameters recorded at one
time) the gap appeared to carry $0.485$ bits about the next panel, yet
conditioning PPM's context on the gap \emph{cost} it $0.047$ bits per event,
because 3600 distinct panels fragment every context it needs. At the
granularity of a single parameter the same conditioning gains PPM $0.18$ bits.
An advantage that appears under one individuation and not under a better-posed
one is not an advantage of the learner, so we use one token per measurement.

\subsection{Codelength, as an instrument}

appears in the first decile and holds: $0.73$ in the first tenth of the seen
contexts, $0.76$--$0.83$ in every later one. On events whose order-2 context the
counter has never seen, the margin is $+0.26$ ($5.57$ against $5.83$ bits). This
reading is taken inside a counter's frame, and \S\ref{sec:measure} states what
it is for: a control that cannot accumulate, against a memory that does.

\subsection{The routed operator graph}

An earlier design passed the state through a learned transform chosen by content
from a small-world graph. With the graph kept, every fact is maintained as
without it, and the walk alone answers a relation slightly worse ($5.10$ against
$4.64$ bits, the same configuration otherwise). On the synthetic stream every
reading falls (Latin $0.486\to0.455$, walk support $0.379\to0.327$, answer bits
$3.98\to4.14$). On the clinical stream the charge rises from $3.319$ to $3.366$
bits per event, and the margin on new contexts falls from $0.26$ to $0.15$. We
removed it because it maintains no fact the mechanism lacks and costs on every
other reading, not because of the charge. A stream that reported which graph
node the state last passed through belonged to it; with the graph removed it
reported a constant, and when repaired to report the last bank written it raised
the charge ($3.343$) and changed nothing on the synthetic stream. It is off.

\subsection{Anchoring the state to the background}

The mechanism pulls $p$ toward the background bands at each tick (anchor
$0.35$). Without the pull every fact is maintained; the walk alone answers a
relation better ($4.64$ against $4.87$ bits) and a new pair slightly worse
($0.24$ against $0.18$), and the synthetic readings are within seed variation
of the mechanism's. The facts do not decide this pair, and we keep the anchor
for the reason it was introduced: a state with nothing tying it to elapsed time
drifts wherever its own walk takes it.

\subsection{A read-back check on the emission}

Before an answer is said, the check unbinds it back through the key it was
retrieved under and refuses it if it does not return. It is equal to the
mechanism in the order of facts; on the synthetic stream it lowers Latin from
$0.486$ to $0.455$ and raises answer bits from $3.98$ to $4.08$. An earlier
measurement of it on the clinical probe was its main support, and that
measurement was on a question \S\ref{sec:clinical} shows to be an order-free
count. It is off.

\subsection{A gate on silent hops that return to what was just heard}

A silent hop can collapse onto the token just heard, which lets go of what
preceded it. The gate refuses such hops. It was built on an accuracy reading
of F9 that proper scores reversed (\S\ref{sec:measure}): the silence improves an
ungrounded answer rather than worsening it. With the gate the improvement is
lost ($+0.001$ against $-0.058$, still inside the threshold), and composition of
a new pair worsens ($0.24$ against $0.18$). On the clinical stream it lowers
the charge, from $3.319$ to $3.290$ bits per event, and most on new contexts
($5.57$ to $5.48$); the negative band of Table~\ref{tab:assoc} narrows ($-0.97$
to $-0.78$ after one occurrence). That is the strongest case in this paper of a
gain on the instrument. When the gate was first built, an explanation of its gain was tested --- that the gate preserves
order information through long silences --- and was not supported: on a
history question with silences of eleven ticks it moved the answer by about one
standard error. A change we cannot explain is not adopted for its charge. It is
off.

\subsection{What was said, bound into what followed}

The mechanism binds what it said into the key of what the world did next
(Eq.~\ref{eq:said}). On every source in this paper this is neutral: the
closeout without it is within $0.005$ of the mechanism on every reading, the
facts are identical, and on the clinical stream $131358$ of $1.86$M triples carry
a said token, at no measurable cost. It is in the mechanism because it is the
only path by which a consequence of the model's own response can be learned,
and no source here has consequences (\S\ref{sec:closing}). Its first version
wrote the said token unmarked into the world's key space, where it collided with
the key of a silent walk and broke a finished walk's hold on its answer
(Table~\ref{tab:failures}); the said token now carries a self marker and an
identity space of its own.

\subsection{An exact trace per context}

A dialogue source (Appendix~\ref{app:screens}) showed that the mechanism has
nowhere to read ``what this context bound to $X$'' from: the superposed store
holds what $X$ is usually bound to. An exact trace, keyed by the pair and
overwritten on write, answers the question: F11 rises from $0.12$ to $0.75$,
and nothing else changes. It is off because it is a transcript. The design's
commitment about context (\S\ref{sec:philosophy}) is that the present is a
context, not a record, and an exact overwrite-on-write store is the record that
commitment refuses. The fact it maintains is real; the question it raises is
whether that fact belongs to memory of this kind or to a separate faculty.

\subsection{Situated memory}
\label{sec:situated}

The question the episode trace raised has a form that does not need a
transcript: bind each triple to the situation it happened in, and read with the
situation reinstated, as the temporal context model does
\citep{howard2002distributed}. The situation is the first level of the world's
own background cascade, unitarised so that binding by it is invertible; the
situated triples go into a superposition of their own, beside the main store.

None of the real sources has situations that recur, so the source for it is
built to have them: four or eight backgrounds,
eight frequent pairs whose successor depends on the situation, and 32 rare
pairs, visited in random order with gaps between visits. Recall is scored as
the cosine of the unbound value with the right successor, situated against
plain (Table~\ref{tab:situated}).

Three things hold on both seeds. Situated recall is three to four times plain
recall on the frequent pairs, whose successor depends on the situation. It is
robust to the gap: after ten or more intervening visits it is close to what it
is after none. And a situation that is reassigned --- the successors redrawn
every eight visits --- is relearned: recall of the new successor passes the
old one on the first visit after the change ($0.42$ against $0.23$ and $0.41$
against $0.18$) and on seed 7 the charge on it falls from $1.58$ to $0.41$ bits.

What does not hold is one-shot recall. A rare pair seen once in a situation is
recalled at $0.10$--$0.22$, barely above plain recall ($0.16$), and reaches the
frequent pairs' level only after six exposures. This is the fan effect
\citep{anderson1974retrieval}: every other situation's writes of the same pair
load the same bank, and a single exposure is one trace among all of them. The
remedy biology uses is pattern separation, sending similar inputs to distinct
codes \citep{yassa2011pattern}. We tried its hard form, selecting a sub-bank by
sign bits of the situation. It made recall worse (later in a visit
$0.80\to0.70$; after a long gap $0.78\to0.53$), because the situations of this
source are not stable enough to be separated: two visits to one situation are
only $0.56$ alike, against $0.06$--$0.09$ for two different situations, so the
bits disagree between visits to the same place.

On the static sources the situated store is neutral in the order of facts and
on the synthetic stream, and it costs $0.034$ bits per event on the clinical
stream ($3.353$ against $3.319$), where there are no situations to recur. The
charge moving the wrong way is not why it is off --- it is off because no source
here has what it needs. An environment with persistent surroundings is the
test it waits for (\S\ref{sec:closing}).

\subsection{Other switches}

Cleanup restricted to tokens actually heard, and the codebook term of the
write restricted to them, were hypotheses for the difference in
Table~\ref{tab:step}; both leave the synthetic readings where they were, and are
off. Step rules that shrink the step as a row settles are reported in
\S\ref{sec:floors}; neither is adopted.

The discipline this forces, stated because we expect it to generalise to any
system of this shape:

\begin{enumerate}
\item Any change on the path of a load-bearing mechanism gets that mechanism's
off-control \emph{in the same sitting}. If the full arm and the off arm are
close, stop.
\item A terminal metric cannot debug a multi-step chain. Four-second unit tests
found errors that ten-minute end-to-end runs could not.
\item Bit-identical arms are more alarming than bad numbers.
\item Any hand-set constant must be back-solved from its own units.
\item Suspect the data before the mechanism, and the instrument before either.
\item Before reading a number as the quantity wanted, ask what else it could
be. Four readings in this project were something else: a trend on a subset
that was the learning phase, a class that was a phase, a descent in progress
read as a floor, and accuracy on answers with no information in them.
\item A synthetic source built with symmetries --- uniform marginals, no shared
structure --- hides every defect those symmetries make invisible. Each real
source found at least one.
\end{enumerate}

Performance belongs in the same list, because where computation is bought with
the world's time it is not merely engineering. The $O(d^2)$ convolution became
$O(d\log d)$, which is also what makes the unitary codebook affordable.

\section{What static data cannot test, and what would}
\label{sec:closing}

\subsection{Every evaluation here is open-loop}

In every experiment of this paper the world's next event is fixed before the
model says anything. Questions put to the model are answered by the world
whatever the model answered; the clinical outcome is spoken at the end of a
stay whatever the model expected; the synthetic source proceeds on a schedule.
Such evaluations test memory --- what is stored, what is retrieved, how it
degrades --- and prediction. They cannot test the response half of the design:
what is said, and what the world does because it was said.

This is not a shortcoming of the particular sources. It holds for any recorded
stream, because a record is precisely an answer that does not depend on the
circumstances of asking --- asking again, who asks, what was asked before, what
is asked together --- and a behaviour has a record model only if it is public
and undisturbed \citep{yang2026gluing}. A learner whose behaviour changes what
it subsequently experiences disturbs its world, so it is not described by any
record of its inputs, and the order in which it asks shapes what it can learn:
along questions that do not commute, each path is a martingale, unasked
branches are never revealed, and what is learned in the limit depends on the
strategy of asking; only in the exchangeable case is it
strategy-independent \citep{yang2026gluing}. That is
the situation a challenge--response learner is built for, and it is the one a
replayed file removes. Nor can a replayed file test time as a resource: the
model receives exactly the ticks the file specifies however much computation it
needed, and is never late.

\subsection{What a world must have}

The properties below are what the preceding sections needed and could not get.
Each is stated as a property of the world, not of a benchmark.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{A clock of its own.} The world proceeds while the model is silent;
thinking longer costs the chance to act or to notice. Only then is depth bought
with silence a trade rather than a free lunch.
\item \emph{Influence without generation.} What the model does changes what it
experiences next --- where it is, what it can see, what reacts --- but the
world's own processes continue regardless. This is what excludes the dark room:
a learner cannot make the world quiet by hiding from it.
\item \emph{Laws that recur across situations.} The same regularities hold in
many places and objects, so that what is learned here can shorten learning
there. Without shared structure, organisation has nothing to organise and
transfer is impossible by construction.
\item \emph{Consequences as events.} The world's reactions are further tokens,
charged and written like any other; nothing is a label and nothing is a score.
\item \emph{Partial observability.} Where to look and what to do decide what is
learned, so that the model's own strategy is part of the experiment.
\item \emph{Situations that persist.} An environment with stable surroundings,
so that ``being here again'' is recognisable. The situated store of
\S\ref{sec:explore} was limited by exactly this: our synthetic situations were
random samples of tokens and two visits to one of them were only $0.56$ alike.
\end{enumerate}

A simulated physical environment has all six by default. Its laws are known to
the experimenter because the experimenter wrote them, which gives a reference
for reading what the learner has organised without handing the learner any
answer; it contains no reward, and none is to be added; and its processes run
whether or not anything attends to them. A discrete lattice physics with
objects, collisions and gravity keeps the laws while avoiding pixels and
continuous control, so that the difficulty lands on memory and response rather
than on perception. Richer simulations, and eventually physical robots, are the
later rungs of the same ladder.

\subsection{Why anything would move, without a reward}

A learner with no reward still has to move, and an organism plainly does not sit
still. This section says what would move it, and it is easy to misread, so we
state first what it is not. It is not a reward: there is no scalar, nothing is
summed over time, nothing is maximised or predicted as a return, and nothing
reaches the write rule. It is not reward shaping, an intrinsic-motivation bonus,
or an objective such as free energy restated: each of those is a single quantity
the learner is trained to optimise, and the design has none. The claim of this
paper, that the learner is reward-free, holds in what follows without
qualification.

What an organism has that a corpus lacks is a body that is part of the world.
Pain, satiety, cold and fatigue happen to it. We call these \emph{valenced
events}: events that the world, through the body, produces, and that the
learner treats like any other. The design's commitment is that they enter in two
places only, as the world's tokens and as a constraint on what is said, and
never as a training signal. We read that as three constraints.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Valence is experienced as events.} Pain, satiety, cold and fatigue
are tokens from the body --- part of the world --- charged and written like
anything else. Memory comes to hold what tends to precede pain because pain is
an event, not because it is bad; the write rule does not know the difference.
How deeply a painful event is written is decided, as for any event, by how
surprising it was.
\item \emph{Valence acts on expression, not on storage.} Among the responses
maturing in parallel, the one to say is chosen with regard to what it is
expected to lead to: a response whose walk through memory reaches pain is held
back, one that reaches relief is let through. Looking further ahead is walking
further along the chain, so how far the learner foresees consequences is bought
with silence like any other depth. The choice changes what is done now and
never what is stored. To keep it from becoming a value function in disguise,
only the first valenced event on a walk is consulted, and nothing is summed.
\item \emph{Drives are several and incommensurable.} Hunger, pain and cold do
not share a currency. Keeping them apart keeps valence from becoming the single
scalar the design refuses, and makes the trade-offs between them a partial
order like the one we measure learners by: summing them into one number would be
the choice of a linear extension that nothing in the world determines
\citep{yang2026gluing}.
\end{enumerate}
The difference from a reward is therefore structural, not a matter of degree. A
reward is a scalar that learning is organised to increase; here memory is
organised by what happened, whether it hurt or not, and valence only selects,
at the moment of speaking, among responses that memory has already matured. If
every valenced event were removed, the learner would still learn everything it
learns now, and would merely have less reason to act one way rather than
another.
A few fixed reflexes --- withdrawing from pain, orienting to a sudden event ---
give behaviour a seed before memory holds any consequence to recall, and drives
that rise on their own clock, like hunger, guarantee that the learner cannot
stay still for long. Designing these, and designing the world, are engineering
problems; the constraints above are what the mechanism asks of them. The
marked keys of Equation~\eqref{eq:said} are the memory such a learner would
learn its own consequences from.

\subsection{What would be measured there}

Nothing in such a world has a fixed answer, and nothing should be scored by
one. The instruments of \S\ref{sec:measure} carry over as facts about behaviour:
\begin{itemize}
\item whether the codelength of the world's reaction to the learner's own acts
falls --- a reading of whether it has learned what its acts do;
\item whether regularities learned in one region shorten learning in another
(acquisition that accelerates, which our sources could not show);
\item whether its expectations stabilise, and after how many changes --- the
stabilization level of a question under a recording protocol
\citep{yang2026gluing};
\item how its own order of looking and acting shapes what it learns;
\item whether what it has organised in memory corresponds to the laws the
simulator was written with.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Where it might be useful}

We state these as directions the properties point to, not as claims; none has
been tested.
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Long-running embodied agents.} A robot in an unstructured setting
meets situations that were not enumerated, receives them once and in order, has
no reset and no labels, and has idle time. Continual single-pass writing, a cost
per tick that does not grow with history, and computation that deepens in a
lull match that shape.
\item \emph{Devices with bounded compute.} Sensor nodes and wearables have
power budgets and long quiet periods. A learner whose depth is bought with
silence spends compute when there is time for it, and a fixed per-tick cost is
a fixed energy per event.
\item \emph{Personal and assistive systems.} Learning one person's routines and
places without labels, and recognising a situation when it recurs, is the kind
of situated, long-range memory \S\ref{sec:explore} began to build and could not
finish on a synthetic source.
\item \emph{Processes whose timing is information.} In monitoring streams ---
clinical, industrial, laboratory --- when something is measured carries
information of its own (\S\ref{sec:clinical}). Spending the gaps as computation
rather than as a symbol to condition on is what the design does with time.
\item \emph{Interlocutors in real time.} Conversation is the picture the design
started from. A dialogue partner that thinks while the other is speaking and
speaks when the lull allows is only testable live, with the other party able to
react.
\end{itemize}

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}

\paragraph{Open-loop throughout.} No result here tests the response half of the
design (\S\ref{sec:closing}). What is reported is memory and prediction.

\paragraph{Replay cannot test time as a resource.} The synthetic silences are
authored and the clinical ones are compressed logarithmically and capped. The
model is never late; there is no rate. Binding the compute budget to wall-clock
time is where depth-from-time becomes a genuine constraint, and it has not been
done.

\paragraph{Sources.} One synthetic source, built by us, whose regimes share no
latent structure, so that acquisition cannot accelerate on it by construction;
one real stream, whose one outcome is an order-free summary. Real-data results
are single-seed; synthetic results use two seeds.

\paragraph{One learner, one step.} The readout's delta rule is the only learner,
and one fixed step cannot serve both deterministic structure (which prefers a
large step) and a noisy target (which needs a small one). Separating learning
structure from chasing noise needs a quantity other than the error, and we do
not have it (\S\ref{sec:floors}).

\paragraph{Episodic memory is unfinished.} The situated store recalls a
recurring situation's own successor across long gaps but not from a single
exposure, because every other situation's writes of the same key load the same
bank; hard pattern separation made it worse on our source (\S\ref{sec:explore}).

\paragraph{Caution.} We do not know what these mechanisms do in a world that
answers back, and do not think it can be predicted from here.

\section{Related work}

\paragraph{Distributed binding.} Our store is a superposition read by
correlation, in the holographic \citep{plate1995holographic} and tensor-product
\citep{smolensky1990tensor} traditions, addressed the way sparse distributed
memory addresses \citep{kanerva1988sparse}, and surveyed as hyperdimensional
computing \citep{kanerva2009hyperdimensional,gayler2003vector,%
eliasmith2013build,kleyko2023survey}. Capacity of such stores is analysed by
\citet{plate1995holographic} and \citet{frady2018theory}; our
Proposition~\ref{prop:capacity} is the form we needed, coupling capacity to a
cleanup codebook of size $V$. Classical associative memories
\citep{willshaw1969non,hopfield1982neural,kohonen1972correlation} are the
ancestors of the readout.

\paragraph{Learned versus fixed addressing.} Differentiable memories
\citep{graves2014neural,graves2016hybrid,weston2015memory} and modern
associative layers \citep{ramsauer2021hopfield} learn where things go. Ours is
fixed by construction, following \citet{yang_2026_22161096}: an address that no
later learning can move is what buys freedom from interference, and the price is
retrieval difficulty. Fast-weight schemes
\citep{schmidhuber1992learning,ba2016using} also write associations at inference
time, but inside an autoregressive objective.

\paragraph{Random transforms.} The routed operator graph we removed behaved as a
random feature expansion \citep{rahimi2007random} feeding a linear readout,
which is the regime reservoir computing occupies deliberately
\citep{jaeger2001echo,maass2002real}.

\paragraph{Episodic memory and its biology.} The situated store of
\S\ref{sec:explore} follows the temporal context model, in which an item is
bound to a slowly drifting context and recall reinstates context
\citep{howard2002distributed}, and the complementary-learning-systems account of
a fast episodic store beside a slow one \citep{mcclelland1995complementary}. Its
failure mode is the fan effect, the slowing and weakening of retrieval as more
facts share a cue \citep{anderson1974retrieval}; the remedy biology uses is
pattern separation in the dentate gyrus, which sends similar inputs to
distinct codes \citep{yassa2011pattern}, and our hard, sign-bit version of it did
not survive a source whose situations were unstable.

\paragraph{Continual learning.} The setting and its interference are classical
\citep{mccloskey1989catastrophic,french1999catastrophic}; responses include
regularisation \citep{kirkpatrick2017overcoming}, replay
\citep{lopez2017gradient}, and architectural allocation, surveyed by
\citet{parisi2019continual}. Our position is the allocation one, taken to its
committed form, with no replay and no task boundary.

\paragraph{Compression and prediction.} The ledger is prequential
\citep{dawid1984present,rissanen1978modeling} in the description-length reading
of learning \citep{blier2018description,grunwald2007mdl}, and the identification
of compression with understanding is argued at length by
\citet{solomonoff1964formal} and \citet{hutter2005universal}. What we do not
take is the objective: there is no reward here
\citep{sutton2018reinforcement,silver2021reward}, and codelength is read as an
instrument.

\paragraph{Foundations without a single context.} The measurement stance of
this paper --- facts in place of a score, records versus disturbed behaviour,
prediction as structure plus position, learning limits that depend on the order
of asking --- is developed mathematically in \citet{yang2026gluing}, which drops
the assumption that local data glue into one global object in one context and
makes the failure measurable. We use its statements about learners and
processes; nothing here depends on its set-theoretic base.

\paragraph{Embodiment and the dark room.} That behaviour influences experience
without generating it, and that this is what defeats the dark-room objection, is
argued in the free-energy literature and its critics
\citep{friston2010free,friston2012free,clark2013whatever}; that intelligence
should be judged in a world rather than on a corpus is the embodied tradition's
position \citep{brooks1991intelligence,lecun2022path}.

\paragraph{Autoregressive systems can do these tasks.} A long-context model can
solve retrieval of this shape by induction \citep{olsson2022context}, and
state-space models \citep{gu2023mamba} carry a running state rather than a
window. Neither can spend a lull: computation per token is fixed, and that is
the axis on which this work differs.

\section{Conclusion}

We built a challenge--response memory in which binding and unbinding are the two
halves of one tick, selected by whether the world spoke, in which several
partial responses mature in parallel while the world is quiet, and in which what
the model says is heard back and bound to what followed. In isolation the
mechanism does what the design says: one link per quiet tick, a halt at the end
of a chain, surplus silence harmless. We derived the operating region of its
store, showed that binding inverts exactly only for unitary codebooks, and
showed that locality-sensitive addressing makes a near miss undetectable by
construction.

On static sources the mechanism maintains every fact on our list but one, and
that one belongs to a transcript the design refuses. On a synthetic stream its
load-bearing parts carry what they should; on a real stream it accumulates over
a million events where a bounded context cannot, and loses to a counter only
where the delta rule has not yet arrived. What was built on top of it and left
out is reported with the reason, and in no case was the reason the charge.
Several parts of the design read as neutral or negative on these sources ---
hearing itself, binding what it said, a deep background, a situated store ---
and each is the part a static source cannot exercise, because on a static source
nothing the model says changes anything.

That is the claim we make and the one we cannot yet test. The design is for a
learner in a world that has a clock and that answers back, and it remains
reward-free there: what such a world adds is consequences, which the learner
writes as events, and a body whose valenced events constrain what it says but
never what it stores. Where it should be
judged is in such a world, simulated or physical, and we expect that judgement
to be substantially harder than anything reported here.

\section{Screening sources}
\label{app:screens}

Each source was screened with memoryless judges before a model was run on it
(\S\ref{sec:measure}). None of these numbers involves the model.

\paragraph{Concept-drift streams.} Electricity is sampled every thirty minutes
without exception (coefficient of variation of the gaps $0.000$), and predicting
the previous label scores $0.853$ against $0.752$ for naive Bayes. The stream has
no inter-arrival structure for silence to spend, and a trivial judge dominates:
it cannot show anything about a memory.

\paragraph{Intensive-care streams.} PhysioNet/CinC Challenge 2012
\citep{silva2012predicting}, set-a. Using only \emph{when} measurements were
taken and never their values, in-hospital death is predicted at AUROC $0.616$
(the number of measurements in the last six hours), against $0.637$ for SAPS-I
and $0.626$ for SOFA, which are computed from the values: the observation
process carries information of its own. The outcome itself is an order-free
summary (\S\ref{sec:clinical}), so the source suits next-event structure and
does not suit questions about order.

\paragraph{A process log.} BPI Challenge 2012 \citep{vandongen2012bpi}, 13087
loan-application cases. Structural questions about a case so far --- has an
offer been sent, is the latest offer event a cancellation --- are answered at
$0.95$ or more by the last two events or by counts. Questions about elapsed
time leave room (over seven days since the offer was sent: counts with elapsed
time $0.897$), but they reduce to keeping a clock. Unsuitable.

\paragraph{Task-oriented dialogue.} MultiWOZ~2.2
\citep{budzianowski2018multiwoz,zang2020multiwoz}, 10437 dialogues and about
$6\times10^5$ questions ``what does the user currently want for slot $s$'',
asked after every user turn. The rule ``the last value the user gave for $s$''
answers $0.908$; restricted to the last three turn pairs, $0.677$; the most
frequently mentioned value, $0.579$; the last value either speaker gave,
$0.582$. The question passes the screen --- it needs long-range retrieval by
domain and slot, keeping who spoke --- and is still unsuitable for this design:
about nine questions in ten are literal, a dictionary keyed by speaker, domain
and slot answers those at $0.99$, and no memory can do better than match it
there. What remains, mostly an offer accepted with ``yes'', is too small and
too conventional to test organisation. It is the source that showed the
mechanism had nowhere to read ``what this context bound to $X$'' from, which led
to the explorations of \S\ref{sec:explore}.

\section{Reproducibility}
\label{app:repro}

The implementation is 20{,}250 lines of Rust with no dependencies,
deterministic under a counter-based generator with fixed-order float
reductions. It carries $36$ structural assertions and $6$ source self-tests
that run on every build; two identical runs agree bit for bit. Constraints
A1--A5 and each mechanism claim of \S\ref{sec:isolated} are asserted by a named
test. Every result in this paper was produced by one build of the mechanism as
described, commit \texttt{7927848}; the commands, seeds and raw outputs are listed
with the code.
