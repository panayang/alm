\begin{abstract}
Almost every contemporary sequence learner is autoregressive: it consumes a
prefix and predicts the next symbol, and its computation per symbol is fixed
regardless of how much time the world allows. We report a system built on a
different footing. It is \emph{challenge--response} rather than
next-symbol: one input per tick, one output per tick, and periods in which the
input is the identity and the model keeps emitting. Its philosophy is that
intelligence is an emergence of memory rather than a product of reward, so
there is no reward, no label, and no gradient crossing a memory write; the only
external signal is the codelength of what the world actually said, charged when
it says something. Its background is deliberately imprecise --- a fuzzy
multi-timescale trace rather than an exact context --- and precise context
association is explicitly \emph{not} a goal.

The mechanism has two halves that are inverses of each other, and the tick
already says which is called for: when the world speaks there is something to
combine, so the model \emph{binds} an observation into a superposed memory;
when the world is silent there is not, so it \emph{unbinds} one link out of that
memory. Several partial responses run in parallel; each quiet tick advances
each of them by one link; the answer that has matured is what gets said. Depth
is therefore not a hyperparameter and not a layer count --- it is how long the
world stays quiet.

We verify in isolation that the mechanism does what the design says: a walk
$a_0\!\to\!a_1\!\to\!a_2\!\to\!a_3$ is traversed one link per quiet tick, stops
at its end rather than wandering past it, and surplus silence is harmless. We
derive and confirm the operating region of the superposed store,
$d > 2k\ln V$, and show that binding is only invertible for unitary codebooks
(reconstruction cosine $0.527$ for Gaussian rows against $1.000$ for unitary
ones at $k=1$). On a synthetic continual stream the retrieval occurs and holds
(cursor-to-answer cosine $0.185 \to 0.268$ against a matched control at
$0.015$), and parallel responses are load-bearing (one trajectory collapses to
$0.007$ where four hold at $0.268$).

We are explicit about what this does \emph{not} establish. Several components
measure net-negative on our source, with a single mechanism that explains all of
them, but that mechanism rests on the source's answers being determined by its
cues --- exactly the property of a constructed source most open to doubt. We
therefore rule on none of them. A replayed stream also cannot test the claim the
architecture exists for: in a file, time is an index; here it is meant to be a
resource. Deciding these questions needs real language content carrying real,
non-authored temporal structure, and a compute budget bound to wall-clock rather
than to ticks. What we claim is feasibility at this scale on this source, for a
design that is non-autoregressive at its root and aimed at stateful continual
learning in a world that has a clock.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

\subsection{The frame we are not using}

The dominant frame for sequence learning is autoregression: factorise
$p(x_{1:T})=\prod_t p(x_t \mid x_{<t})$, feed a prefix, predict the next symbol
\citep{elman1990finding,vaswani2017attention}. It is enormously successful and
it carries three commitments that are easy to stop noticing.

First, \emph{the objective is next-symbol prediction}, so every metric derived
from it inherits that shape. Second, \emph{computation per symbol is fixed}: a
forward pass costs what it costs whether the world gives the model a
microsecond or an hour. Third, \emph{the context is a prefix}, an exact and
recent window; long-range association is bought by widening it or by retrieving
into it \citep{beltagy2020longformer,borgeaud2022improving}.

For a system meant to run in a real temporal world --- embodied, or agentic over
long horizons --- all three are uncomfortable. There is no prefix boundary in a
room. Computation that cannot use extra time cannot exploit a lull. And an exact
window is the wrong idealisation for a background that is, in any organism, a
fuzzy multi-timescale trace rather than a transcript.

\subsection{What replaces them}

We take challenge--response as the primitive. There is no ``a challenge
arrives, now respond''; there is a stream, and every tick carries one input and
produces one output. Some inputs are the world's tokens; in the intervals
between them the input is the identity and the model still emits, iterating on
memory. The model's own emissions are part of its context, interleaved with the
world's --- distinguished from them, but combined with them.

The picture we have kept in view throughout is two people talking. While the
other is still speaking you have already begun to think; when they stop, or just
as they stop, you begin to say something. That interval is the gap. And what is
forming in it is \emph{not one trajectory} --- several half-formed replies mature
together and one of them is what comes out. Every one of those clauses turned
out to be load-bearing, and each had to be re-derived after a run in which the
mechanism looked inert (\S\ref{sec:method}).

The philosophy is that intelligence is an emergence of memory rather than a
product of reward. This is not a slogan about scale; it is a design constraint
with teeth. There is no reward signal, no value function, no label. The only
external quantity is the codelength of what the world actually said, charged
only when it says something --- prequential accounting in the sense of
\citet{dawid1984present} and \citet{rissanen1978modeling}, used as a ledger
rather than as a training loss \citep{blier2018description}. A node performs a
memory \emph{write}; it does not perform an autoregressive fit, and no gradient
crosses a write.

We also make a negative commitment that is easy to overlook: \emph{precise
context association is not a goal}. The background is meant to say ``which
memories are relevant here'', not to reconstruct what was said. A design that
tries to make the background exact turns it into a prefix by another name.

\subsection{What we borrow, and from where}

The storage is content-addressed through a fixed map that no later learning can
move, following the position argued in \citet{yang_2026_22161096}: allocation
buys freedom from interference and pays for it in retrieval difficulty, and the
central weakness of the family is that discrimination between addresses decays
as addresses crowd. That trade is the axis along which our design is organised,
and one of our results is that half of the crowding penalty, in our
implementation, was self-inflicted (\S\ref{sec:addressing}).

The representation is holographic \citep{plate1995holographic} in the vector
symbolic tradition \citep{kanerva1988sparse,kanerva2009hyperdimensional,%
gayler2003vector}: binding by circular convolution, reading by circular
correlation. The pairing of an associative store with a small-world routing
graph follows \citet{watts1998collective}; the readout is a delta rule
\citep{widrow1960adaptive}. The continual setting --- regimes arriving,
running, and departing permanently, with no task identifier and no boundary
signal --- is the one in which catastrophic interference was first characterised
\citep{mccloskey1989catastrophic,french1999catastrophic} and to which
complementary-systems accounts respond
\citep{mcclelland1995complementary,kirkpatrick2017overcoming}.

\subsection{Contributions}

\begin{enumerate}
\item A tick in which binding and unbinding are the two halves of one loop,
selected by whether the world spoke, so that gap periods, self-output as
context, and depth-from-time are consequences of one rule rather than three
mechanisms (\S\ref{sec:design}).

\item An isolated demonstration that a chain is traversed one link per quiet
tick, halts at its end, and is unharmed by surplus silence
(\S\ref{sec:isolated}).

\item The operating region of a superposed store, $d>2k\ln V$, derived and
confirmed, together with the observation that binding is only invertible for
unitary codebooks --- a Gaussian codebook loses almost half the signal with a
single item stored (\S\ref{sec:capacity}).

\item A structural result on addressing: locality-sensitive addressing makes a
near miss \emph{undetectable by construction}, because the occupants of a bank
have keys close to the query. Addressing needs same-content-same-bank and gains
nothing from similar-content-similar-bank (\S\ref{sec:addressing}).

\item An explicit statement of what a constructed source cannot settle, and of
why a replayed stream cannot test a model for which time is a resource
(\S\ref{sec:cannot}, \S\ref{sec:limits}).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Design}
\label{sec:design}

\subsection{The tick}

Write $\bind$ for circular convolution, $\unbind$ for circular correlation,
$\nu(\cdot)$ for normalisation, $E_x$ for the fixed codebook vector of token
$x$, and $M_b$ for the $b$-th superposed store.

\paragraph{Every tick carries an arriving token.} When the world speaks it is
the world's token. When the world is silent it is the last thing the world said
--- the relation stays on the table as the operator, and the model's own
emission is where it has got to. This is what removes the need to know whether a
challenge is pending: there is no ``locate the question'', only a running state
and a token per tick.

\paragraph{Speech binds.} On an event tick the standing conjunction of the two
previous observations is combined with what has just arrived and superposed:
\begin{equation}
M_{h(x_{-2},\,x_{-1})} \;\mathrel{+}=\;
\nrm{\big(E_{x_{-2}} \bind E_{x_{-1}}\big) \bind E_{x}} .
\label{eq:write}
\end{equation}
For a presented link $x \; r \; y$ the triple formed at the $y$ tick is exactly
$(E_x \bind E_r)\bind E_y$, and no episode boundary was needed to know that.

\paragraph{Silence unbinds.} On every tick, each of $m$ responses in flight
advances one link:
\begin{align}
q_i     &= \nrm{c_i \bind E_{\text{arriving}}}, \label{eq:key}\\
r_i     &= \nrm{M_{h(\tau_i,\,\tau_{\text{arriving}})} \unbind q_i}, \\
c_i     &\leftarrow \operatorname{clean}(r_i) \quad\text{if the cleanup clears
its threshold.} \label{eq:step}
\end{align}
Here $\tau_i$ is the token identity the $i$-th response has resolved to and
$\operatorname{clean}$ is nearest-neighbour cleanup against the codebook.
Because $\bind$ commutes, a response that has just reached $a_1$ after hearing
$r$ carries the key for the next link without any bookkeeping.

\paragraph{A response that arrives holds; speech supersedes it.} A step that
retrieves nothing fades a response \emph{only if it has never advanced}; one
that has traversed at least one link and then stops has \emph{arrived}, and
fading it would fade the answer. From inside, ``the chain ended'' and ``the
chain never started'' are identical, and the only thing separating them is
whether anything was ever retrieved. Arrival holds through silence; every world
token decays what is in flight before a new response is seeded. Holding a
half-formed reply while the other person is quiet, and dropping it when they say
something new, is the same picture.

\paragraph{The emission reads the most matured response, not their average.}
Averaging unit cursors and renormalising divides each by
$\sqrt{\sum_i w_i^2}$, so the response holding the answer arrives at the readout
diluted to the point of being indistinguishable from noise. ``Several
half-formed answers mature together and one of them is said'' is not ``say
their average''.

\paragraph{The readout.} Features are
$\varphi = [\,c_{\text{best}} \,;\, \text{bound traces} \,;\,
\text{event-locked bands}\,]$, and
\begin{equation}
s(o) \;=\; \langle R_o, \varphi\rangle \;+\; \lambda\,\langle E_o, p\rangle ,
\label{eq:score}
\end{equation}
a learned associative row plus a direct codebook comparison. The second term is
not learned; it is the cleanup memory that lets a retrieved state be
\emph{said}. Rows are updated by a delta rule whose negatives are the tokens the
model currently ranks highest, which is both error-driven and the same
distribution the ledger charged.

\subsection{Structural constraints}

Five constraints are asserted rather than hoped for, and are checked by unit
tests that run on every build.

\begin{description}
\item[A1 Rate limit.] Constant computation per tick. The only way to think
longer is to occupy more ticks, and ticks are given by the world.
\item[A2 Event-driven accounting.] Charged only when the world speaks. ``The
identity input injects no new content'' is then a consequence of the ledger, not
an added axiom.
\item[A3 Read/write separation.] The write walk is unperturbed and content
addressed; reads may wander.
\item[A4 No content write at baseline.] Identity ticks write nothing, asserted
bit-for-bit over 500 baseline ticks.
\item[A5 Timescale reachability asymmetry.] The world writes all rungs; the
model's own output reaches only the fastest, so self-generated content
contributes exactly zero to the slow timescales.
\end{description}

\subsection{The background is deliberately imprecise}

The background is a cascaded band-pass ladder --- one use of a ladder, on the
time axis only; the memory itself is flat, because a ladder inside the store
would be an abuse of the device. The bands are \emph{snapshotted at the last
event}, so the retrieval key stops moving when the world does. Both halves of
that matter. The bands carry which regime this is, which must hold across a
silence, and how long the world has been quiet, which changes every tick; with
both in the key, elapsed time decides whether a memory fires at all. A fact
learned at six ticks of silence did not answer at one.

\section{Isolated verification of the mechanism}
\label{sec:isolated}

The evidence in this section is from unit tests on synthetic memories, in which
every quantity is known. It says what the mechanism does; it says nothing about
whether the mechanism is useful on any particular data. We keep the two kinds of
evidence typographically separate throughout, because conflating them is the
single easiest way to overstate a result of this kind.

\subsection{One link per quiet tick}

Present $a_0 \!\xrightarrow{r}\! a_1 \!\xrightarrow{r}\! a_2
\!\xrightarrow{r}\! a_3$ forty times each, interleaved with unrelated traffic,
then ask with the start and the relation and stay quiet.

Four things had to be right simultaneously for Table~\ref{tab:walk}, and each
was found by a test that runs in four seconds rather than by an end-to-end run
(\S\ref{sec:method}): the relation is the operator and the model's own output
is the position, not the reverse; seeding must retire the \emph{least live}
response rather than the oldest, because on ties ``oldest'' is the slot holding
the response that has just advanced; responses must be weighted by whether they
are still advancing; and the configuration must lie inside the operating region
of \S\ref{sec:capacity}.

\subsection{Capacity, and why binding needs a unitary codebook}
\label{sec:capacity}

A superposition $M=\sum_{i\le k} t_i$ of unit, near-orthogonal triples returns
$\langle \hat M, t_j\rangle \approx 1/\sqrt{k}$ for a stored triple and
$\approx 1/\sqrt{d}$ for one that was never written. Cleanup runs against a
codebook of $V$ entries, and a random vector's best match there is
$\approx\sqrt{2\ln V / d}$. Retrieval is therefore usable exactly when
\begin{equation}
\frac{1}{\sqrt{k}} \;>\; \sqrt{\frac{2\ln V}{d}}
\qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad
d \;>\; 2k\ln V .
\label{eq:capacity}
\end{equation}
Measured at $d=256$: cosine $0.043$ with $k=401$ against a predicted $0.050$.
Equation~\eqref{eq:capacity} is what gives content addressing a job that is not
about accuracy: the address chooses \emph{which} superposition to unbind, each
bank stays under capacity, and the read stays one unbinding however much has
been stored.

Equation~\eqref{eq:capacity} holds only for \emph{unitary} codebooks --- vectors
whose Fourier magnitudes are all one. Correlation is an exact inverse of
convolution only then; for Gaussian rows the reconstruction is lossy before any
interference at all.

\subsection{Addressing must not preserve locality}
\label{sec:addressing}

A key nobody wrote still hashes into an occupied bank, and its occupants answer.
So the end of a chain does not retrieve \emph{nothing}; it retrieves a
neighbour. No threshold on the retrieval separates the two, because both are
real stored content of comparable strength. This is address crowding
\citep{yang_2026_22161096} in its sharpest form.

We built a read-back check for it --- bind the candidate back onto the key and
ask whether that triple is in the bank at all --- and it failed for an
instructive reason. Under sign-bit locality-sensitive hashing
\citep{indyk1998approximate,charikar2002similarity}, the occupants of a bank
have keys \emph{close to the query}; if cleanup snaps to some occupant's target
$y_i$, then for unitary vectors
\begin{equation}
\big\langle \nrm{p_i \bind y_i},\; \nrm{q \bind y_i} \big\rangle
= \langle p_i, q\rangle ,
\end{equation}
and $\langle p_i,q\rangle$ being large is \emph{precisely why they share a
bank}. The verification is defeated by the same property it was built to detect.

The resolution is that addressing needs same-content-same-bank and gains nothing
from similar-content-similar-bank; the second property is pure cost, and it is
what makes a near miss undetectable. Scrambling the code removes the locality
but makes the address brittle, since write and read reach the same convolution
by different floating-point paths and one flipped sign then means an unrelated
bank. That dilemma is false: cleanup has already resolved the response to an
exact codebook token and the write holds both identities, so the address can be
a hash of the \emph{identities}. Vectors carry the superposition; identities
carry the address. With that in place the read-back check becomes inert --- zero
rejections --- because an unwritten key now lands among unrelated occupants
whose contaminated output fails cleanup on its own. We report it as doing
nothing rather than crediting it.

\section{The source and its instruments}
\label{sec:source}

Regimes arrive, run for a while, and depart permanently; there is no task
identifier, no boundary signal, and no reset. Each carries first-order facts, a
Latin square, a product code, composition chains, and walks. Two families can be
answered only by computing: composition queries are posed \emph{once} per chain
and withheld Latin cells are asked once each, so nothing about them can be
memorised from a previous presentation.

The generator is self-tested before any model is built, because on this project
the generator produced as many serious defects as the model did. Its properties,
measured on the emitted stream with no threshold:

An earlier version of this source drew one Latin cue from a Zipf distribution
and the other uniformly. For $T=(a+b)\bmod m$, $P(T\mid A)$ is uniform exactly
when $p_B$ is; so that square was \emph{not} zero-marginal, a predictor using
cue $B$ alone reached $0.2745$, and every Latin accuracy the project had ever
reported was at or below that. Both cues are now uniform, and it has to be
both: no choice of square repairs it, and making both Zipf only makes the leak
symmetric. The frequency skew now lives outside the square, where it forges no
single-cue shortcut. Marginal information is estimated with the Miller--Madow
correction \citep{miller1955note}.

Table~\ref{tab:exposure} also exposes a representational fact. A novel product
pair scores $0.000$ where cue $A$ alone would give $0.5$: $E_a \bind E_b$ is a

fresh random vector uncorrelated with anything stored. We have the
\emph{combine} half of distinguish-and-combine; the parts are not recoverable
downstream by the learned readout. That is precisely the gap the unbinding half
of \S\ref{sec:design} exists to close, and closing it is what moved composition
from $0.015$ to $0.331$ in Table~\ref{tab:negatives}.

\section{What this source cannot settle}
\label{sec:cannot}

Three components measure net-negative here: the ladder feedback of the model's
own channels, the operator graph, and background depth beyond one rung. One
mechanism explains all three, and the arms order monotonically by it --- and we
rule on none of them, for a reason we consider more important than the numbers.

\paragraph{The hypothesis.} $\varphi$ is a fixed-width retrieval key. Anything
added to it that is not a function of the fact being asked about adds variance
without signal. On this source the answer to a Latin item is determined by its
two cues, so what the model itself just said carries no information about it,
while making $\varphi$ differ between two presentations of the same fact. The
operator graph is near-random for the same reason its learning does nothing:
each edge receives ten to forty rank-one updates against $4096$ parameters, and
its drift grows as the square root of the update count, the signature of
uncorrelated accumulation. Background bands are context, not fact.

\paragraph{Why we do not rule.} Every one of these rests on the source's answers
being determined by its cues --- the property of a constructed source most open
to doubt. The hypothesis makes a sign-flip prediction: on a source where the
answer is \emph{not} cue-determined and the model's own trajectory carries
necessary information, the sign should reverse. The only family here with that
property is the walk family, whose cells hold six to nine events.

\paragraph{The three-stream requirement has two implementations with opposite
signs, and must not be reported as one.} Ladder feedback carries no signal here
and is net-negative. Cursor advance --- the model's own output as the position a
chain has reached --- is load-bearing (Table~\ref{tab:cursor}). The founding
requirement that input and output streams interleave as context is met by the
second; the first is one way of writing it that happens to hurt here.

\paragraph{A test we abandoned.} We proposed settling this by measuring the
variance of $\varphi$ across repeated presentations of the same fact. It has
almost no power: 2304 dimensions, four to nine presentations per fact, and a
rank correlation between two noisy orderings. Two difference tests replace it
--- excluding a component from $\varphi$ while leaving it in the dynamics, and
substituting a noise block of matched width and variability --- but both belong
on real data (\S\ref{sec:limits}) rather than on a source already known not to
settle them.

The discipline this forces:

\begin{enumerate}
\item Any change on the path of a load-bearing mechanism gets that mechanism's
off-control \emph{in the same sitting}. If the full arm and the off arm are
close, stop.
\item A terminal metric cannot debug a multi-step chain. An end-to-end zero has
no power to say which of five steps broke: four-second unit tests found four
errors where ten-minute end-to-end runs found none.
\item Bit-identical arms are more alarming than bad numbers.
\item Any hand-set constant must be back-solved from its own units. Three of our
failures were constants that had never been checked against the scale they
operate on.
\item Suspect the data before the mechanism. The generator produced as many
serious defects as the model, and each was first read as ``the mechanism does
not work''.
\end{enumerate}

Performance work belongs in the same list, because in a design where computation
is bought with the world's time it is not merely engineering. Replacing an
address that regenerated 4096 random keys per call with pre-generated sign bits
took one arm from over twenty-one minutes to $6$m$43$s; the $O(d^2)$ convolution
became $O(d\log d)$, which is also what makes the unitary codebook affordable.

\section{Limitations, and what a real evaluation requires}
\label{sec:limits}

\paragraph{A replayed stream cannot test a model for which time is a resource.}
Our silences are drawn from $\{1,2,4,8\}$ --- authored, not observed. The model
is never late: it receives exactly the ticks the file specifies regardless of
how much computation it needed. There is no rate, and in a real continual
setting the ratio between the world's event rate and the agent's compute rate is
decisive. Replay can test mechanism; it cannot test the claim this architecture
exists for, that computation and the world share a clock.

\paragraph{The autoregressive baseline is the wrong yardstick, and the right
lower bound.} We report PPM \citep{cleary1984data} not as a standard to beat ---
that would be adopting the next-symbol objective as the measure --- but as a
control that \emph{cannot accumulate}: a bounded window cannot carry anything
across itself, so a sustained gap is evidence of accumulation. On charged
events, order-4 PPM costs $11.80$ bits at accuracy $0.008$ and scores exactly
$0.000$ on both conjunction families, against $9.17$ bits and $0.206$/$0.732$
for the mechanism at the time of that comparison. The variant handed the
segmentation the mechanism refuses to assume reaches $5.60$ bits, and losing to
that is the comparison worth losing.

\paragraph{Our source has no transferable structure.} Each regime carries its
own square, its own entities, its own product code, and shares nothing with any
other. Transfer is therefore impossible by construction, and an
acquisition-acceleration measurement --- is the $n$-th regime learned faster
than the first? --- is guaranteed flat. If a constructed source is retained, the
one change worth making first is shared latent relations across regimes, because
that is the minimum condition under which organisation has anything to organise.

\paragraph{Attention is empty without laws.} Letting the model's state influence
which of the concurrently live regimes speaks next --- $P \propto
\exp(\beta\cdot\text{align})$, with $\beta=0$ recovering the present source
exactly --- is only meaningful when the same regularity recurs across
situations, so that attending here teaches you about there. The world's own
progression must stay exogenous: things happen whether or not the agent attends.
That single property is what stops the ledger from being gamed by an agent that
finds a quiet corner, which is the failure mode of a fully closed loop
\citep{friston2012free}. Real reactions are influenced by the individual without
being generated by it.

\paragraph{What would settle these questions.} Real language content carrying
real, non-authored temporal structure: timestamped transcripts, subtitle
streams, conversation logs --- data in which silences are real silences of real
duration, and in which the two-people-talking picture is the data rather than an
analogy. A tick then corresponds to a unit of real time, and the natural next
step is to bind the compute budget to wall-clock, at which point depth-from-time
becomes a genuine constraint and the efficiency of an implementation becomes
part of its capability. This is the direction in which
\citet{lecun2022path} and the embodied tradition \citep{brooks1991intelligence}
both point, and it is where a system built on this footing should be judged.

\paragraph{An explicit caution.} We do not know what these mechanisms do on real
data, and we do not think it can be predicted from here. Intelligence is not a
quantity our source is capable of putting under strain. The negative results of
\S\ref{sec:cannot} are consistent with a plausible mechanism; they are not
verdicts, and we have tried throughout to write them as what they are. Every
result in this paper is single-seed unless stated, and the families that cannot
be memorised carry sixty to ninety-six events in total.

\section{Related positioning}

Our store is a superposition read by correlation, in the holographic and vector
symbolic tradition \citep{plate1995holographic,kanerva2009hyperdimensional},
addressed the way sparse distributed memory addresses
\citep{kanerva1988sparse}. Differentiable memories such as the Neural Turing
Machine and its successors \citep{graves2014neural,graves2016hybrid} and modern
associative-memory layers \citep{ramsauer2021hopfield} learn their addressing
end-to-end; ours is fixed by construction, following the argument of
\citet{yang_2026_22161096} that an address no later learning can move is what
buys freedom from interference. Fast-weight schemes
\citep{schmidhuber1992learning,ba2016using} write associations at inference
time as we do, but within an autoregressive objective.

The random-transform component of our graph turned out to behave as a random
feature expansion \citep{rahimi2007random} with a linear readout, which is the
regime reservoir computing occupies deliberately \citep{jaeger2001echo}; we
report this as a negative structural finding rather than a design.

The continual setting and its interference are the classical ones
\citep{mccloskey1989catastrophic,french1999catastrophic,kirkpatrick2017overcoming},
and the two-store intuition behind allocation is the complementary-systems one
\citep{mcclelland1995complementary}. Our accounting is prequential
\citep{dawid1984present,rissanen1978modeling,blier2018description}; our stance
that compression and understanding are the same currency is the one argued at
length by \citet{hutter2005universal}. What we do not take from the surrounding
literature is the objective: there is no reward here
\citep{sutton2018reinforcement}, and the founding position that intelligence
emerges from the organisation of memory rather than from optimisation against a
scalar is the one this system was built to take seriously
\citep{hebb1949organization,tulving1972episodic}. That an autoregressive model
with a long context can solve retrieval tasks of this shape by induction
\citep{olsson2022context} is not in dispute; what it cannot do is spend a lull.

\section{Conclusion}

We built a challenge--response memory in which binding and unbinding are the two
halves of one tick, selected by whether the world spoke, and in which several
partial responses mature in parallel while the world is quiet. In isolation the
mechanism does what the design says: one link per quiet tick, a halt at the end
of a chain, surplus silence harmless. We derived the operating region of its
store, found that binding is only invertible for unitary codebooks, and found
that locality-sensitive addressing makes a near miss undetectable by
construction --- so that half of the crowding penalty this family is known for
was, in our implementation, self-inflicted.

On a synthetic continual stream the retrieval occurs, holds, and requires
several trajectories rather than one. Several components measure net-negative,
with one mechanism that explains them; we rule on none of them, because the
explanation rests on a property of the source rather than of the world, and
because a replayed file cannot test a system for which time is a resource. What
we claim is feasibility at this scale on this source, for a design that is
non-autoregressive at its root, that treats precise context association as a
non-goal, and that is aimed at stateful continual learning in a world that has a
clock. Where it should actually be judged is on real language carrying real
time, and we expect that judgement to be substantially harder than anything
reported here.

\subsection*{Reproducibility}

The implementation is 8953 lines of Rust with no dependencies, deterministic
under a counter-based generator with fixed-order float reductions, and carries
20 structural assertions and 6 generator self-tests that run on every build.
Two identical runs agree bit-for-bit. The structural constraints A1--A5 and the
mechanism claims of \S\ref{sec:isolated} are each asserted by a named test.
